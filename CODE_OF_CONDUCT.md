# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss technical behavior and
evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, or deliberate disruption. Respect privacy, licensing, and vendor-
neutrality boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for unacceptable behavior. Enforcement
decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and applied
consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a contact
method listed on the owner's GitHub profile. Do not publish personal or
sensitive details in an issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical.
